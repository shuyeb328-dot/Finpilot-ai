# FinPilot Asset & Strategy Coverage

FinPilot now has a unified architecture for:

- Personal finance and cash management
- Public equities, ETFs, mutual funds and bonds
- Options, futures and derivatives
- FX and commodities
- Crypto and DeFi
- Real estate and REITs
- Private companies, venture/private equity concepts and M&A
- Business strategy and competitive intelligence
- Historical strategy/conflict case studies
- Macro, rates, inflation and central-bank analysis
- Insurance, taxes, accounting and treasury

All modules feed the same evidence store, probability engine, risk engine and round-table approval process.
