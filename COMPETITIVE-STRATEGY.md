# Competitive Strategy & Business-War Room

FinPilot includes a legal, ethical competitive-strategy layer for business decisions. It can analyze competition using public/authorized information and historical strategy frameworks.

## Strategy library
- Competitive positioning and differentiation
- Cost leadership, focus, premium positioning
- Market-entry and market-defense scenarios
- Pricing, bundling, packaging and channel strategy
- Capacity, supply-chain and inventory resilience
- Negotiation, BATNA, signaling and commitment strategy
- Game theory: prisoner’s dilemma, repeated games, credible commitments, signaling
- Moats: brand, switching costs, network effects, scale, data, distribution, IP
- Blue-ocean / category creation
- Disruption and response planning
- Competitor reaction trees and second-order effects
- M&A, partnerships, joint ventures and strategic alliances
- Capital allocation and war-chest planning
- Crisis communications and reputation defense
- Red-team / pre-mortem / post-mortem analysis

## Guardrails
The system must not facilitate violence, sabotage, cyberattacks, extortion, fraud, market manipulation, insider trading, anticompetitive collusion, or evasion of law. Competitive recommendations should use lawful public/authorized information and surface legal/compliance review when relevant.
