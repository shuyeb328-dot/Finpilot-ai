# Approval Governance

Approval is evidence-gated, not popularity-gated.

Suggested tiers:
SMALL -> automated policy check + owner confirmation
MEDIUM -> division review + CEO judge
LARGE -> full round table + risk/compliance + human approval
CRITICAL -> independent human/board/legal/regulatory approval as applicable

Any conflict, missing critical evidence, stale data, policy breach or model anomaly can force HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
