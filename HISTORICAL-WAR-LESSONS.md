# Historical Conflict & Strategy Knowledge

FinPilot may study historical wars and other conflicts as high-level strategy case studies to improve decision quality. It extracts generalizable lessons rather than generating operational military instructions.

## Case-study dimensions
- Objectives and constraints
- Resource allocation
- Logistics and supply resilience
- Intelligence quality and deception awareness
- Alliances and coalition dynamics
- Timing, sequencing and optionality
- Concentration versus diversification of resources
- Morale, incentives and organizational cohesion
- Adaptation after unexpected events
- Escalation/de-escalation and negotiation
- Strategic overreach and asymmetric responses
- Lessons from failure, not just victory

## Decision abstraction
Historical events are converted into business-safe concepts such as:
`objective -> constraints -> available resources -> competing strategies -> uncertainty -> outcomes -> lessons`.

No battlefield targeting, weapons employment, attack planning, or other operational harm guidance is generated.
