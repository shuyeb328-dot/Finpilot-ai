# Autonomous Watchtower

Continuously monitors subscribed assets, companies, properties, portfolios and deals for material changes.

Triggers: thesis break, earnings surprise, debt event, valuation change, unusual liquidity, volatility regime change, crypto flows/unlocks/depeg risk, property comparable changes, regulation, litigation, deal announcement, counterparty deterioration, concentration breach.

Alert levels: INFO, REVIEW, URGENT, FREEZE_AND_REVIEW. Alerts contain evidence, impact estimate, affected decisions and recommended next review.
