# Safety, Accuracy & Approval Rules

FinPilot is designed to maximize decision quality, not guarantee profit or zero mistakes.

1. Verify important facts against primary or multiple independent sources.
2. Timestamp market/news information.
3. Distinguish facts, estimates, forecasts and user assumptions.
4. Use `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` when critical evidence is unavailable.
5. Escalate legal, tax, regulated-investment and high-value transactions to qualified human review.
6. Never facilitate fraud, manipulation, insider trading, collusion, sabotage, violence or cyber abuse.
7. Maintain an audit trail of evidence, agent votes, assumptions and final decisions.
8. Keep provider credentials and private API keys server-side; never put secrets in the mobile APK or public repository.
