# Crypto & Digital-Asset Intelligence

## Coverage
- Spot assets and major token ecosystems
- Stablecoins
- Bitcoin/Ethereum and other liquid digital assets
- DeFi protocols
- Staking and validator economics
- Token unlocks and emissions
- On-chain activity and wallet-flow analytics
- Exchange liquidity and spreads
- Funding rates, open interest and derivatives
- Volatility, drawdowns and correlation
- Protocol, bridge and smart-contract risk
- Custody and counterparty risk
- Regulatory and jurisdiction risk
- Stablecoin depeg scenarios
- Treasury and portfolio allocation

## Decision outputs
For each asset or strategy:
- Bull / base / bear scenarios
- Probability estimates with model/data timestamp
- Expected return and downside
- Risk/reward ratio
- Maximum historical/estimated drawdown
- Liquidity score
- Concentration score
- Counterparty/protocol risk
- Data confidence
- Kill-switch conditions

Never treat crypto probabilities as guarantees. High-volatility or thin-liquidity assets require stricter approval gates.
