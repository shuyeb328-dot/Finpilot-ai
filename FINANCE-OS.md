# FinPilot Finance OS

FinPilot is expanding from an investment decision engine into an all-round finance operating system.

## Coverage
- Personal finance: net worth, budgeting, cash flow, debt, emergency runway, goals
- Investing: valuation, portfolio risk, capital allocation, paper execution, market intelligence
- Business finance: company intelligence, deal verification, negotiation, capital planning
- Real assets: real estate and crypto assessment
- Tax planning: reserve estimation with jurisdiction/professional disclaimer
- Risk & governance: security division, approvals, audit trail, evidence gates
- Intelligence: watchtower, learning, multi-agent round table

## Rule
No module may invent live financial data. External feeds must identify source/freshness, and live money movement remains disabled by default.
