# Simulation Lab

Runs Monte Carlo, scenario trees and stress tests over portfolio/company/property/crypto digital twins.

Scenario families: normal, bull, bear, crash, inflation shock, rate shock, FX shock, liquidity freeze, crypto drawdown, property downturn, revenue decline, refinancing stress, counterparty failure, regulatory change.

Each run records assumptions, random seed, model version, distributions, outputs, confidence intervals and sensitivity drivers. Simulations are decision support, not forecasts with certainty.
