# FinPilot AI — 8 Major AI Executive Structure

FinPilot uses an 8-division AI organization. Each division has specialist agents, receives the same verified evidence packet, and can challenge other divisions in a round table.

## 1. CEO / Final Decision AI
Owns the final decision record. It does not invent facts. It reconciles votes, evidence quality, valuation, risk, probability, and strategic fit.

## 2. CFO & Financial Intelligence AI
Accounting, cash flow, capital structure, unit economics, budgeting, financial statements, treasury, liquidity and scenario planning.

## 3. Investment & Valuation AI
Stocks, funds, bonds, private businesses, crypto, real estate and deals. Produces intrinsic value, market value, downside value, expected value and scenario-weighted return.

## 4. Risk & Security AI
Market, liquidity, credit, counterparty, operational, cyber, fraud, concentration, regulatory and tail risks. Performs stress tests and red-team attacks.

## 5. Markets & Macro AI
Rates, inflation, FX, commodities, equity markets, crypto markets, liquidity cycles, central-bank policy and macro regimes.

## 6. Business & Strategy AI
Competitive strategy, pricing, market entry, negotiation, game theory, moat analysis, partnerships, M&A and strategic response planning. Historical conflict material is used only for high-level strategy lessons, not operational violence.

## 7. Assets & Real-World AI
Real estate, REITs, infrastructure, commodities, businesses and other tangible assets. Covers cash yield, financing, cap rates, occupancy, development and liquidity.

## 8. Compliance, Tax & Governance AI
Tax analysis, regulatory checks, suitability, policy constraints, governance, auditability, source verification and approval controls.

# Shared specialist layer
Every division can call specialist agents such as Bull, Bear, Quant, Fundamental, Macro, Crypto, Real Estate, Deal, Tax, Legal-Review, Fraud, Red-Team, Evidence and Scenario agents.

# 7 control agents
1. Evidence Controller — source quality and freshness.
2. Data Controller — normalization, missing values and provenance.
3. Risk Controller — exposure limits and stress testing.
4. Valuation Controller — independent valuation challenge.
5. Probability Controller — calibrated probabilities and uncertainty.
6. Decision Controller — checks rule compliance and conflicts.
7. Audit Controller — immutable decision/audit record.

# 6 decision gates
DATA → EVIDENCE → VALUATION → RISK → PROBABILITY → CEO APPROVAL.

No gate may silently be skipped for a material decision.
